---
name: Brand direction
description: User direction for the FTSM website’s visual tone and readability.
---

Keep the website creative but more business / professional. Avoid the original bright yellow.

**Why:** The user said, “the yellow doesn't read well. Need something creative but more business / professional.”

**How to apply:** Preserve this direction when updating the website, advisor pages, and future brand materials. Prefer readable, restrained accents rather than returning to the original neon palette.
