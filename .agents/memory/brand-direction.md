name: Brand direction
description: User direction for the FTSM website’s visual tone and readability.
---

Direction "Marquee" (approved Oct 2026): warm near-black background (#0F0E0C), cream text (#F4EFE7), one muted amber accent (#F2A93B). Condensed uppercase headlines in Big Shoulders Display; body in Manrope. Logo: three rising bars (stairs that read as a growth chart) — first two cream, the tall third bar amber.

The user earlier rejected a bright neon yellow for readability; the muted amber on black was chosen explicitly as its replacement. Keep it restrained: amber is the only accent, used for emphasis, buttons and the First Movers band.

Positioning: Daniel is "an operator, not a consultant" — copy should say what FTSM does, not what it "identifies" or "helps with".
